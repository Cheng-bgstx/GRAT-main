# Failed or Retired Hypotheses

- Amplitude-only versus phase-only attacks did not show stable separation.
- Direct spectral modulation of Gaussian perturbations often weakened attacks.
- Earlier feature FFT analysis used incorrect non-spatial axes in part of the historical code.
- Fisher/task-basin geometry did not explain transfer gains.
- Source decoder observability did not control medical decoder sensitivity; TOTD is retired as the main proposal.
- Corrected ACRS showed weak structure but failed its incremental Dice-prediction gate on 2026-10-11.
